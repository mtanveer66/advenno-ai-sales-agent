# Advenno AI Sales Agent — Use Cases

Real-world problems the platform solves.

## Agency New-Business Pipeline

Marketing, branding, and creative agencies need a steady flow of prospects. The agent keeps the top of the funnel full automatically, so business development never goes quiet.

## B2B Outbound Sales

Sales teams lose hours to list-building and manual research. The agent handles discovery, enrichment, and first-touch outreach so reps focus on conversations and closing.

## Founder-Led Sales

Early-stage founders can run real outbound without hiring an SDR — define the target market, and the agent does the prospecting.

## Lead-Generation Services

Companies that sell lead lists can deliver enriched, scored, ready-to-work leads at scale instead of raw scraped data.

## Local Service Businesses

Businesses targeting a specific geography and industry (e.g. "dentists in a metro area") can build and contact a complete prospect list quickly.

## Market Research & Territory Mapping

Use the discovery and enrichment layers to understand who's operating in a market before committing sales resources to it.
