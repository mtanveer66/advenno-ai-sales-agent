# Advenno AI Sales Agent — Features

A detailed look at what the platform does. (Marketing documentation — no implementation details.)

## Multi-Source Lead Discovery

The agent finds business leads from a range of discovery sources:

- Google Maps
- Facebook
- Foursquare
- Geoapify
- Additional business directories

This breadth means a fuller, more representative list of prospects in your target market.

## AI Lead Enrichment

Raw leads are thin. The agent enriches each one with additional business intelligence drawn from multiple data providers — so your team has real context before the first message.

## Smart Lead Scoring

Not every lead is worth the same effort. The agent scores and ranks leads by how well they fit your offer, so your team works the highest-value opportunities first.

## AI-Personalized Outreach

Generic templates get ignored. The agent generates outreach messages tailored to each lead, dramatically improving response rates over one-size-fits-all blasts.

## Multi-Channel Sending

Prospects respond on different channels. The agent reaches them across:

- Email
- WhatsApp
- SMS

## Command Center Dashboard

A single web interface to run scraping jobs, manage enrichment, launch outreach campaigns, and monitor results.

## Reliability at Scale

- API key rotation across data sources keeps campaigns running.
- Built to handle outbound at volume, not just one-off lists.

## Exports

Lead lists export to spreadsheet formats, ready for your CRM or team workflow.
