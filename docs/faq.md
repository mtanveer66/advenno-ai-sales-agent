# Advenno AI Sales Agent — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is the Advenno AI Sales Agent?**
An AI-powered lead generation and outreach platform that automates discovery, enrichment, scoring, and multi-channel contact.

**Who builds it?**
Advenno — a full-stack brand-building studio. See [advenno.com](https://advenno.com).

## Lead Discovery

**Where do leads come from?**
Multiple business-discovery sources, including Google Maps, Facebook, Foursquare, and Geoapify.

**Can I target a specific industry and location?**
Yes. You define the target market, and the agent builds the matching prospect list.

## Enrichment & Scoring

**What is lead enrichment?**
Augmenting each raw lead with additional business intelligence so your team has context before outreach.

**How does lead scoring work?**
Leads are ranked by how well they fit your offer, so the best opportunities surface first.

## Outreach

**What channels does it support?**
Email, WhatsApp, and SMS.

**Are messages personalized?**
Yes. Outreach is AI-generated and tailored per lead, not sent as generic templates.

## Data & Exports

**Can I export leads?**
Yes. Lead lists export to spreadsheet formats for use in your CRM or by your team.
